# Printer Not Printing

## Symptoms

- Printer is not printing the user's document.
- Printer is displaying as offline.
- Print job is stuck in the queue.
- Printer is unable to print for other users.

## Possible Causes

- Printer is offline or disconnected.
- Ran out of paper or ink/toner.
- User selected the incorrect printer.
- Printer driver may need to be updated.
- Experiencing network connection problems.

## Resolution Steps

1. Confirm the printer is powered on and connected.
2. Look for any error messages on the printer.
3. Check paper and ink/toner status.
4. Check if the user selected the correct printer on their computer.
5. Check if the printer is showing as online.
6. Clear the print queue and print a test page.
7. Restart the printer and retry to confirm it is working.

**Guide:** [Fix printer connection and printing problems in Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/hardware/printer/fix-printer-connection-and-printing-problems-in-windows)

## Escalation

Escalate the issue if:

- Printer still won't work after troubleshooting.
- Printer has a hardware error.
- Other users are also unable to print.
- Printer is unable to connect to the network.
- Admin access is needed.
