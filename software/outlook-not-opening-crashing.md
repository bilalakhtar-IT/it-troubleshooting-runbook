# Outlook Not Opening/Crashing

## Symptoms

- Outlook won't open, freezes, or crashes randomly.
- Outlook becomes very slow or unresponsive when sending or opening emails.
- Issue is only occurring on the user's computer and not on their other devices.

## Possible Causes

- Outlook add-ins causing issues.
- Outlook profile may be invalid or corrupted.
- Microsoft Outlook may need a repair or update.

## Resolution Steps

1. Restart the computer and relaunch Outlook.
2. Disable Outlook add-ins:
   - Open Outlook.
   - Click File > Options.
   - Select Add-ins on the left side.
   - At the bottom, next to Manage: COM Add-ins, click Go.
   - Uncheck the add-ins you want to disable.
   - Click OK.
   - Close and reopen Outlook to check if the issue is resolved.

**Guide:** [Turn an add-in off for Outlook for Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/turn-an-add-in-off-for-outlook-for-windows)

3. Create a new Outlook profile and reconfigure the user's profile:
   - Close Outlook completely.
   - Open Control Panel and search for Mail.
   - Click Mail (Microsoft Outlook) > Show Profiles.
   - Click Add, enter a profile name, and click OK.
   - Enter the user's email/account information and complete the setup.
   - Select Always use this profile and choose the new profile.
   - Click Apply > OK, then open Outlook and test.

**Guide:** [Create a profile in Outlook for Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/create-an-outlook-profile)

4. Check for available Outlook updates and install them if needed.
5. Relaunch Outlook and confirm the issue is resolved.

## Escalation

- Basic troubleshooting doesn't resolve the issue.
- Outlook continues to freeze or crash.
- Creating a new Outlook profile does not resolve the issue.
- Advanced troubleshooting or admin access is needed.
